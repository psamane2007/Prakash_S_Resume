For employees

Requesting time off

  1. Select Time Off from the main menu.
  2. Click Request Time Off.
  3. Choose the leave type (Vacation, Sick Leave, Personal Day, etc.).
  4. Pick your start and end dates. For a partial day, choose Half day and select morning or afternoon.
  5. Add an optional note for your manager, such as handoff plans or an urgent-contact option.
  6. Review the total days requested and click Submit.

Check your remaining balance first under Time Off > Balances.

After you submit

  • Your manager is notified and will approve or decline.
  • You’ll be notified by email / in-app once it’s reviewed.
  • Track the status (Pending, Approved, Declined) on the Time Off page.

Changing or canceling

  • Pending: open the request and select Edit or Cancel.
  • Approved: select Request Change or contact your manager.

For managers

Reviewing requests

  1. You’ll receive a notification when someone on your team submits a request. You can also open Time Off > Team Requests at any time.
  2. Open the request to see the employee, leave type, dates, total days, and any note.
  3. Check Team Calendar to see who else is out during those dates.
  4. Click Approve or Decline.
  5. If declining, add a reason so the employee knows what to do next (for example, suggest alternate dates).

Things to know

  • Response time: Requests should be reviewed within X business days. Reminders / auto-escalation apply if a request is left pending.
  • Balances: Approving a request deducts from the employee’s balance automatically. You can see their remaining days on the request.
  • Approved requests that change: If an employee asks to modify or cancel approved time, you’ll get a new notification to review.
  • Out of office: If you’re away, delegate approvals to another manager under Settings so requests aren’t stuck.
